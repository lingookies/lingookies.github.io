💡 The Secret to Sounding Native

When you only study single words, you end up translating English logic directly into Italian. This leads to awkward pauses and confusing mistakes. The secret that polyglots use to speak faster is mastering collocations – words that naturally belong together.

❌ English Logic vs. ✅ Italian Logic

In English, you "take" a shower.
In Italian, you "make" a shower.

In English, you "pay" attention.
In Italian, you "lend" attention.

In English, you "are" right or wrong.
In Italian, you "have" right or wrong.
